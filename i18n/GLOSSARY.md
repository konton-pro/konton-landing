## Tone notes
- Warm, human, concise, slightly poetic; never corporate jargon.
- Adapt meaning and rhythm, not words; keep headline length close to the pt-BR original (headlines are layout-sensitive).
- Plain international English: no US-only idioms. Spelling is British-neutral ("modernisation"); stay consistent.
- Keep `{br}`, `{em}`, `{slash}`, `{day}`, `{month}`, `{year}`, `{time}` placeholders exactly; split headlines so `titleEm` is the emphasised ending.
- English drops the pt "h" time suffix (`{time} · online`).
- Translate alt texts and aria-labels too; describe the image, don't repeat the caption.
